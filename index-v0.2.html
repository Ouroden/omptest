01 · overview

A prequel set before the crash landing

In the main game, engineers crash land with a rocket. In Nullius, you play the android sent ahead to make that crash landable.

no coal / oil / wood / burners no free O₂ renewables first asteroid mining android duplicates no pollution near-dormant enemies

Biters still exist — evolution and expansion run at time_factor 0.0000002, so the colony can grow before they notice it.

Terraform first, factory second

In this Factorio prequel, you play an android sent to terraform barren planets and seed them with life. Eons later your efforts will result in a galaxy full of planets ready for engineers to crash land on.

This is a full overhaul mod that replaces all recipes and technologies. No life means no coal, no oil, no wood, and no free oxygen in the atmosphere — so there is no burner technology either.

Built on light elements

Since many planets are poor in rare heavier elements like copper or uranium, your technology will focus on the most abundant, lighter elements.

Advanced resources such as copper and uranium only become available later, once asteroid mining technology is reached.

Duplicate yourself

You can build duplicates of yourself and control multiple android bodies to accomplish tasks more quickly.

One mind, many hands — the first step toward an industrial base large enough to launch rockets.

A clean void

The map is generated without the pollution system — Nullius forces the map setting off in data.lua:

data.raw["map-settings"]["map-settings"].pollution.enabled = false

Your challenge is scarcity, not smog.

02 · resources

Seven fundamentals

The fundamental natural resources are Iron Ore, Sandstone, Bauxite, Calcite, Air, Seawater, and Volcanic Gas. Everything else is manufactured — or mined off asteroids.

Iron Ore

ore

The backbone of the early game: iron feeds smelting, steel, and the machines that build everything else.

Sandstone

ore

Provides silicon — essential for electronics and glass — plus trace quantities of titanium ore.

Bauxite

ore

An ore for aluminum, a useful electrical conductor and structural material.

Calcite

ore

A source of calcium, useful in cement, glass, and metallurgy — and a source of trace amounts of sulfur.

Air

gas

Mostly nitrogen and carbon dioxide — a critical feedstock for organic chemistry products like plastic — with traces of other important gases, including noble gases like argon and helium.

Seawater

fluid

A source of hydrogen, oxygen, chlorine, sodium, and trace amounts of deuterium, tritium, lithium, and other minerals.

Volcanic Gas

gas

A source of sulfur, carbon monoxide, and trace amounts of boron. Pumps it up; vents it safely.

Copper

advanced

A heavier element drawn from asteroids once asteroid-mining technology is reached — feeds wiring and electronics.

Uranium

advanced

Harvested from asteroids after asteroid-mining tech; fuels the fission endgame of the energy ladder.

03 · energy

No burners. Ever.

Without coal or free oxygen, there is no burner technology. You rely on a blend of renewable energy sources, unlocked in stages.

earliest

Wind

The earliest source: intermittent, and turbines have to be spaced out. The factory learns to breathe with the weather.

next

Solar

A slightly more advanced alternative — with the usual day–night cycle, so output follows the sun.

steady

Geothermal

The first steady source of energy, but it may only be placed in limited volcanic locations.

advanced

Deuterium–tritium fusion

At higher technology levels there is nuclear power, starting with fusion of isotopes harvested from seawater.

endgame

Uranium fission

Eventually uranium fission — once asteroid mining is unlocked and the heavy elements arrive.

Storage is the real puzzle

Wind and solar require energy storage, but without heavy elements, batteries require moderately advanced technology.

Prior to unlocking batteries you will need other energy storage strategies — including stored hydrogen/oxygen to burn during periods of low energy production, and compressed gas energy storage.

Renewable by design

No life means no free oxygen, requiring varied renewable energy sources.

Every megawatt in Nullius traces back to the wind, the sun, the planet's heat, or the isotopes in its water.

Achievement — "Easy Breezy": put your first wind turbine to work within 45 minutes of the game starting. — Nullius achievement

04 · endgame

Seed the world with life

Once you've established a sufficient industrial base to launch rockets, the game stops being about production — and starts being about biology.

Raise the oxygen level

The atmosphere has no free oxygen. You will need to raise its oxygen level before a living ecosystem can hold.

Reestablish contact with your progenitors

You must reestablish communications with your progenitors to download genomes of the species you will seed.

Build biology from scratch

Assemble biological materials from scratch until you have a sufficient breeding stock to reproduce itself naturally — a genetically diverse ecosystem of multiple plant and animal species, each with their own survival requirements.

Let the factory farm life

Many of these species produce useful materials more cheaply than you can manufacture them, so you may wish to integrate some of these organisms into your factory production lines.

Send your duplicates out

Launch some of your duplicates to repeat this process on other planets throughout the galaxy — the world you built becomes the cradle for the engineers who crash land on it.

Eons later your efforts will result in a galaxy full of planets ready for engineers to crash land on. — the nullius premise, mod portal

05 · difficulty

Two ways to make it harder

The content mods Lambent Nil and Tricky Old Nick extend Nullius with new basic resources and modify recipes to use them.

Lambent Nil

v3.2.5 by half_a_cat 2.86K downloads Factorio 1.1–2.0 MIT

A Nullius extension that integrates phosphorus chemistry and extends the electronics production chain. Phosphorus — a rare resource refined from phosphorite rock — is used in electronics, biochemistry, and chemical separation of exotic elements.

Carries the Fluorapatite startup setting: when enabled, fluorine is added to some recipes for an increased challenge. Off by default. → Try the toggle
Mod portal Codeberg source

Tricky Old Nick

v1.2.7 by half_a_cat 2.40K downloads Factorio 1.1–2.0 MIT

The rare metal nickel — an easily refined conductive, corrosion-resistant material — underpins production as the android sets out to terraform the planet. With technological advances, nickel is displaced by aluminum and steel, yet it remains essential for niche applications due to its distinctive magnetic, heat-tolerant, and chemical properties.

Makes the early game more challenging — recommended for players who like to play with many ores, or who want a replay with a fresh challenge at the outset. Incompatible with alignment mode — it can be disabled in the Nullius settings if you need aligned factions. Co-op plays fine.
nullius ≥ 2.0.4 + lambent-nil ≥ 3.2.4 — layers on top of Lambent Nil
Mod portal Codeberg source
One install, whole chain: adding Tricky Old Nick pulls in Lambent Nil ≥ 3.2.4, which pulls in Nullius ≥ 2.0.4 — all three mods activate.

06 · fluorapatite

One switch, more chemistry

Lambent Nil's startup setting lambent-nil-fluorine — displayed in-game as Fluorapatite — "adds fluorine to some recipes for an increased challenge". It ships off by default, and being a startup setting it's chosen when the world is created.

bool-setting · startup

Default: false. Defined in Lambent Nil's settings.lua; try the switch to see what changes in the production picture.

What changes

Lambent Nil's phosphorus chain is always there. The switch decides whether fluorine enters the chemistry.

Phosphorus chain
phosphorite rock → phosphorus fluorine → electronics biochemistry exotic-element separation

Refined from phosphorite rock; feeds electronics, biochemistry, and the chemical separation of exotic elements.

Calcite carries fluorite
calcite → calcium + fluorite traces

With the setting on, calcite also carries traces of fluorite alongside its calcium.

Fluorine byproducts
fluorine → refrigerants fluoropolymers

Production and cleanup of these byproducts couples into other resource flows.

Fluorine stays out
fluorapatite → fluorine → not required

With the setting off, the standard phosphorus chain runs without fluorine requirements.

07 · settings

Tuned at world creation

Nullius exposes seven startup settings; Lambent Nil adds one more. All are startup settings — chosen when the world is created. Search to filter the list.

settingmodtype defaultrangenotes
nullius-alignment nullius boolean on — Multiplayer alignment: shared research for everyone, or independent factions + alignment cards. (Tricky Old Nick requires this off.)
nullius-wind-turbine-energy-multiplier nullius double 1.0 0.01–100 Wind turbine output scale. Hidden from the in-game settings UI.
nullius-hide-void-alt nullius boolean on — Hides the alternate vent icons (Chimney / Outfall).
nullius-hide-recipe-signals nullius boolean off — Hides recipe signals not shown by default.
nullius-match-wind-turbine-activity-sound nullius boolean on — Turbine sound follows turbine activity.
nullius-wind-turbine-volume nullius double 1.0 0–1 Wind turbine sound volume.
nullius-pump-energy-multiplier nullius double 10 0.1–1000 Pump electricity multiplier.
lambent-nil-fluorine lambent nil boolean off — "Fluorapatite" — phosphorite also yields fluorine, calcite carries fluorite traces, feeding refrigerants & fluoropolymers.
